# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mr. James Chicken serves both international travelers visiting Phong Nha and local residents. Visitors are deciding where to eat and need to quickly understand the food, trust the restaurant, find it, and feel motivated to visit in person.

## Product Purpose

The website should make the restaurant feel worth a visit, build confidence through real food, heritage, reviews, pricing, and practical information, and ultimately entice customers to come to the store and try the food.

## Positioning

Mr. James Chicken combines Mr. Gabriel's more than 30 years of cooking experience, dating to 1984, with a convenient Phong Nha location. The restaurant's enduring craft and its place in the local travel context are the durable differentiators.

## Operating Context

People commonly evaluate the restaurant while already in or traveling through Phong Nha, often on a phone and potentially while planning around cave trips or local activities. They need to scan the menu and prices, understand opening hours, see trustworthy customer feedback, and navigate to the store.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the menu, current prices, opening hours, address, phone contact, directions, dine-in and takeaway information, and in-page menu filtering.
- The primary conversion is an in-person store visit; menu exploration and directions should support that outcome.
- The experience must work well for both travelers and local residents, especially on mobile.
- Existing factual claims must remain accurate and must not be embellished with invented awards, sourcing claims, or service promises.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mr. James Chicken Phong Nha.
- Mr. Gabriel's 30+ years of cooking experience and the "since 1984" heritage are central, verified parts of the story.
- The Phong Nha location is a central part of the restaurant's identity and value.
- Existing logo and heritage artwork are available in `public/images/`, but the raster logo currently prints “Since 1994,” which conflicts with the confirmed 1984 date. Do not display that date-bearing artwork until it is corrected.

## Evidence on Hand

- The displayed customer reviews are real and current.
- The 4.9 rating is real and current.
- The menu prices are real and current.
- Opening hours, address, location, and the "since 1984" claim are real and current.
- Existing food photography, logos, and map artwork are available in `public/images/`.

## Product Principles

- Make the food immediately desirable and the visit feel easy.
- Lead with real proof: food, prices, reviews, experience, hours, and location.
- Treat heritage as lived credibility, not decorative nostalgia.
- Serve travelers and locals with equally clear, practical information.
- Optimize the journey from first impression to directions and an in-person visit.
